Product Types

From basic floor tools to fully outfitted janitorial carts, we stock everything your cleaning crew needs.

🧹
Wet Mops

Professional-grade mop heads for daily wet mopping on hard floors. Available in cotton, microfiber, and rayon.

  • Cotton, microfiber & rayon yarn
  • 12 oz to 32 oz weight range
  • Loop-end and cut-end styles
  • Color-coded options available
🪣
Brooms & Brushes

Indoor and outdoor sweeping tools for all surface types — lobby floors, parking lots, kitchens, and more.

  • Push brooms 18"–36" sweep face
  • Counter brushes & hand brushes
  • Deck scrub brushes
  • Corn brooms & lobby brooms
🪣
Buckets & Wringers

Heavy-duty mop buckets and wringer systems for efficient floor cleaning with minimal mess.

  • 26 qt to 44 qt bucket capacity
  • Side-press wringer systems
  • Down-press wringer systems
  • Combo bucket-wringer units
🛒
Janitor Carts

Fully equipped housekeeping carts for hotels, hospitals, and commercial facilities to carry all supplies in one pass.

  • Housekeeping & maid carts
  • Room attendant carts
  • Open & enclosed shelf designs
  • Heavy-duty plastic construction
🪟
Floor Squeegees

Channel squeegees and floor squeegees for moving water quickly on smooth concrete, tile, and sealed floors.

  • 18", 24", 30", and 36" widths
  • Rubber and neoprene blade options
  • Straight and curved channel styles
  • Compatible with standard handles
🧽
Dustpans & Scoops

Lobby dustpans, counter dustpans, and heavy-duty scoops for dry debris collection in any facility.

  • 12" to 36" sizes available
  • Lobby long-handle dustpans
  • Counter-style and wall-mount
  • Metal and poly construction
