Equipment Categories

What We Sell

Every category of commercial floor care and cleaning equipment — from walk-behind scrubbers to backpack vacuums — backed by our in-house repair team.

🤖
Walk-Behind Scrubbers

Cylindrical and disc scrubbers for hard floor cleaning in mid-size facilities — schools, retail, healthcare, and warehouses.

  • 17" to 32" scrub width
  • Cylindrical & disc pad options
  • Battery (AGM/Lithium) or propane
  • Solution & recovery tanks onboard
🚗
Ride-On Scrubbers

Large-facility ride-on scrubbers for warehouses, distribution centers, airports, and big-box retail with wide open floor space.

  • 28" to 40" scrub width
  • Lithium battery option available
  • Onboard solution & recovery tanks
  • Traction drive — operator comfort seat
⚡
Buffers & Burnishers

Low-speed buffers for scrubbing and stripping, and high-speed burnishers for restoring a high-gloss finish to floor wax.

  • 175–350 RPM buffer range
  • 1,500–2,000 RPM burnisher range
  • 20", 21", 24", 27" pad sizes
  • Propane burnishers available
🧹
Upright Vacuums

Heavy-duty commercial upright vacuums for carpet and hard floor maintenance in offices, hospitality, and commercial buildings.

  • 12" to 18" cleaning path
  • HEPA filtration standard
  • Bagged and bagless models
  • Onboard tool storage
🎒
Backpack Vacuums

Ergonomic backpack vacuums for fast, efficient cleaning of stairs, aisles, offices, and areas where uprights can't easily navigate.

  • 4 to 7 quart capacity
  • HEPA filtration
  • Adjustable harness system
  • Ideal for multi-floor facilities
💧
Carpet Extractors

Portable and walk-behind carpet extractors for deep cleaning carpets, upholstery, and fabric in commercial and healthcare settings.

  • 5 to 20 gallon tank capacity
  • Heated & unheated models
  • Portable spot extractors available
  • Walk-behind for large carpet areas
