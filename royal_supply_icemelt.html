Ice Melt Products

From economy rock salt to extreme-cold calcium chloride — we carry every level of ice management for Minnesota winters.

❄️
Multi-Melt™ Ice Melter

Royal Supply's proprietary blend — our most popular product for Minnesota facilities. Balanced performance, safe on concrete when used as directed, and blue-tinted for easy visibility of application areas.

  • Proprietary blended formula
  • Effective to –25°F
  • Blue tinted for application visibility
  • Concrete-safe when applied as directed
  • Available in 50 lb bags & bulk pallet
🧊
Calcium Chloride

The fastest-activating ice melter available. Exothermic reaction — generates heat on contact and begins melting immediately even in extreme cold. Best for urgent black ice situations.

  • Fastest activation of any ice melter
  • Exothermic — melts on contact
  • Effective to –25°F
  • Flake and pellet form available
  • Use sparingly — very concentrated
🌊
Magnesium Chloride

A gentler alternative to calcium chloride — lower corrosivity on metal, safer around vegetation, and lower toxicity for pets and wildlife. Good choice for entryways and landscaped areas.

  • Effective to –13°F
  • Lower toxicity than calcium chloride
  • Gentler on vegetation & metal
  • Flake and pellet form available
  • Pet-friendlier option
⛏️
Rock Salt (NaCl)

The most economical ice control option — ideal for large surface areas like parking lots and access roads where cost matters. Not suitable for extreme cold or concrete-sensitive applications.

  • Most economical per pound
  • Effective to +20°F
  • Best for large paved areas
  • 50 lb bags and bulk available
  • Not for concrete or vegetation areas

Best Practices

Application Tips

Get maximum effectiveness and minimum product waste with proper ice melt technique.

🕐
When to Apply

Apply before temperatures drop below 25°F when possible. Pre-treating pavement before a storm event dramatically reduces the product needed and prevents ice bonding.

🔄
Pre-Treatment vs. Reactive

Pre-treatment with liquid or pellet product at 1–2 oz/sq yard prevents bonding. Reactive treatment after ice forms requires 3–5x more product. Pre-treat whenever the forecast allows.

📦
Storage Tips

Store ice melt in a cool, dry location in sealed bags or covered bins. Calcium chloride is hygroscopic and will absorb moisture from the air if left open — causing clumping and reducing effectiveness.
