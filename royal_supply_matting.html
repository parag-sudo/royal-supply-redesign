What We Carry

Product Types

From entrance mats that stop dirt at the door to floor finish programs that keep hard floors looking their best.

🚪
Entrance Mats

Scraper/wiper combination mats, custom logo mats, and roll goods to trap dirt, moisture, and debris before it reaches your floors.

  • Scraper, wiper, and combo styles
  • Custom logo mats available
  • Roll goods sold by the linear foot
  • Standard sizes: 3'×5', 4'×6', 3'×10'
🦶
Anti-Fatigue Mats

Ergonomic standing mats for workstations, assembly lines, reception desks, and retail counters — reducing leg and back fatigue for standing workers.

  • 3/8" to 3/4" thickness
  • Foam, gel, and rubber core options
  • Beveled safety edges
  • Grease-resistant options for kitchens
🏗️
Outdoor Scraper Mats

Heavy-duty rubber and vinyl scraper mats built to withstand Minnesota weather — snow, salt, and mud at entryways, loading docks, and ramps.

  • Heavy-duty rubber or vinyl
  • Drainage holes for water runoff
  • Aggressive scraper surface
  • UV-resistant, weather-rated
✨
Floor Finish & Wax

Professional acrylic floor finish in high-gloss and matte options. 1–5 coat application programs for VCT, linoleum, and sealed concrete.

  • High-gloss (20–25% solids)
  • Matte finish available
  • 1 to 5 coat application program
  • Compatible with spray buff
🧪
Floor Stripper

Aggressive strippers for removing multiple coats of built-up floor finish. No-scrub and scrub-assisted formulas for use with floor machines.

  • No-scrub & scrub formulas
  • High-emulsifying stripper
  • Use with neutralizer & rinse
  • 1 gal and 5 gal sizes
🏢
Floor Restorer & Cleaner

Daily neutral cleaners, spray buff restorers, and traffic lane cleaners to maintain floors between strip-and-refinish cycles.

  • Neutral pH daily cleaner (pH 7)
  • Spray buff restorer
  • Traffic lane cleaner
  • Concentrates to 1:64 or higher
